What is the difference between rooter service and hydro jetting?+

Rooter service uses a cable with cutting heads to break through clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high pressure water to scour the full inside of the pipe, clearing grease and buildup and leaving the line flowing like new. For tough, recurring clogs, jetting is the longer lasting fix.

Can you find a blockage without digging?+

Yes. Our camera inspections let us look inside the pipe and pinpoint cracks, blockages, and root intrusion, so we diagnose accurately and fix the issue without unnecessary digging or damage.
